Entry for the make.fun bounty **One Word for the Boss**: send exactly one
word describing the boss. The program writes that word to
`/output/entry.txt` — nothing else.

## The word

**leech** — a boss who lives off your work and drains your energy.
Chosen by local judging: two independent judges, position-swapped
pairwise rounds in two rounds against the field's top words (seagull,
hovercraft, circlebacker, arsonist, bottleneck, delegator, ghost).
It had the highest headline of all free candidates (lowest-judge
estimate 0.586, combined 0.614), the smallest spread between the two
judges, and at 5 bytes it also holds the tie-break. It wins on truth —
everyone has met a boss who takes the credit and the energy — while
staying funny on its own.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt containing: leech
```
